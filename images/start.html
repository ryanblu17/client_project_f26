Starting Your Experience

Turn Plans Into Partnership

Once you've prepared, it's time to make first contact with your partner. This guide helps you launch a professional working relationship and set your project up for a strong start.

Why a Strong Start Matters

The beginning of a project is when ideas become shared commitments. Clear communication, a well-organized kickoff meeting, and written agreements help build trust and keep everyone aligned from the start.

Starting Goals

  • Partner communication: Draft clear, professional outreach to launch the relationship.
  • First meetings: Structure a kickoff meeting that defines roles and project scope.
  • Formalizing agreements: Put team commitments and scope in writing so everyone is aligned.

Featured Starting Resources

  • Initial Client Email Examples: Templates for first contact with a client or partner.
  • Initial Client Meeting Sample Agenda: Structure for a professional kickoff call.
  • Student Org-ELO Partnership Agreement Template: Formal agreement outlining team/ELO roles.
